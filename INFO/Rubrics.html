Description of each of the major components (10%) 

Clear explanation of the problem and objectives. Clear explanation of each of the major aspects of the product including a rationale for each of the design and implementation decisions. 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Description of the development lifecycle (5%) 

Clear evidence of the application of an appropriate software development methodology. Discussion of each of the major stages including how validation and verification were applied at each stage. 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Critical analysis and reflection (5%) 

What went wrong and what was right? What could be done differently next time? Appraisal of the product; analysis of approach taken (with hindsight); analysis of software/tools used.
